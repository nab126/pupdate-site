The puppy potty‑training log.

Log potty trips, meals, and naps in two taps — or by voice, transcribed right on your device. pupdate learns your puppy's rhythm and reminds you when the next trip is likely due, tracks your training progress, and celebrates milestones along the way.

Share the log with your household so everyone stays in sync, and export it anytime to show your vet. No accounts, no ads, no tracking — your data lives on your device and your own iCloud, where we can't see it.

Why pupdate exists

A few months ago, my partner and I adopted Lily — a 12-week-old Italian Greyhound. Everyone warned us about potty training a puppy. They were right.

We kept a shared Google Doc of every potty trip, meal, and nap: timestamps, guesses, bleary 3am notes. It worked, but keeping it up was exhausting — and the notes never told us the one thing we actually wanted to know: when does she next need to go out?

Lily is fully potty trained now. The logging shouldn't have been the hard part, so I built the app we wish we'd had: two taps to log, one shared record for the whole household, and your puppy's rhythm made visible.

pupdate is free — if it spares one other household the 3am spreadsheet, it was worth building.

— Nabeel, and Lily 🐕
